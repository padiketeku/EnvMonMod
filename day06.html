Day 6 · Monday 9 November: SAR, floods and crocodiles

Theme. Week 2 starts with radar. You map surface water and floods with Sentinel-1 and test whether floodplain inundation explains crocodile biomass.

  • Map wet-season flooding with Sentinel-1 (ULO 3, 4).
  • Test a species–habitat relationship with a multi-river model (ULO 1, 3).
