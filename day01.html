Day 1 · Monday 2 November: Getting started: Earth Engine, image processing and your study tile

Theme. You set up Earth Engine, learn how satellite images are managed, corrected and enhanced, and receive the personal Daly River tile you will use in every assessment.

Today you will:

  • Explain why remote sensing is central to landscape ecology, and what each sensor family (optical, SAR, lidar) can measure (ULO 1).
  • Apply the main image processing steps: scaling, cloud masking, registration, enhancement and transformation (ULO 2).
  • Build an NDVI time series and describe savanna phenology (ULO 4).
  • Run prac00 and record your tile, years and AT4 settings (ULO 4).

Before class:

  • Read article review notes 1.1 (Foody, 2023) and 1.3 (Skidmore et al., 2021): about 20 minutes. They are the "Read first" for Prac 01.
  • Bring a laptop and a Google account you can use for Earth Engine.
